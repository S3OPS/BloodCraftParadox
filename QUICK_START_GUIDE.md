## The Core Principle

### BEFORE (Purple Prose):
"The night of the full moon arrived with theatrical grandeur..."

### AFTER (Visceral Reality):
"Full moon. I'd been awake since noon. Useless."

**Why it works:**
- Immediate (Full moon.)
- Physical detail (awake since noon)
- Honest reaction (Useless)
- No purple prose
- Reader IN the moment

## Quality Check (Use After Each Change)

Ask yourself 5 questions:
1. **Showing or telling?** → Must be showing
2. **Could I cut an adjective?** → If yes, cut it
3. **Is dialogue natural?** → Would real person say this?
4. **Would film director know what to shoot?** → Must be specific
5. **Explaining or experiencing?** → Must be experiencing

If any answer is wrong → revise

## The Pattern (Memorize This)

### For DESCRIPTIONS:
```
BAD:  "The city sprawled before us in impossible splendor, its ethereal beauty..."
GOOD: "The smell hit first. Magic and exhaust. A million heartbeats."
```
Lead with SMELL or SOUND → Specific details → Ground in body

### For DIALOGUE:
```
BAD:  "I assume your mother elaborated on the profound nature of..."
GOOD: "Tell me about the city." / "Nocturne's different."
```
Short exchanges → Fragments okay → Subtext over explanation

### For EMOTION:
```
BAD:  "I felt terrified as the transformation began..."
GOOD: "My hands wouldn't stop shaking. Cold sweat on my neck."
```
Never name the emotion → Show physical reaction → Let reader infer

### For ACTION:
```
BAD:  "The world dissolved around me in a cascade of sensation..."
GOOD: "The world dissolved. I was particles. Colors that hurt. Hunger watching."
```
Fragment for speed → One image per sentence → Create urgency

## Example: Complete Transformation

### ORIGINAL (Purple Prose):
```
The transition through the portal was nothing like I expected. I had imagined 
something dramatic—a flash of light, a sense of displacement, perhaps even 
physical discomfort. Instead, it was like walking through a curtain of cool 
silk. One moment we were in The Crimson Door; the next, we emerged into an 
entirely different reality.

Nocturne sprawled before us in impossible splendor. The city existed in a 
perpetual twilight, its sky a deep purple canvas adorned with stars that 
seemed too bright, too close. Three moons hung overhead—one silver, one gold, 
one a deep crimson that cast everything in shades of blood and shadow.
```

### TRANSFORMED (Visceral):
```
Portal. Cool silk against my skin. Step through—

Wrong gravity. Wrong air. Wrong light.

Nocturne didn't sprawl. It hunched in the valley like something wounded and 
dangerous. Three moons overhead—silver, gold, crimson. The red one pulsed. 
Or maybe that was my blood singing to it.

The smell hit first. Magic and exhaust. A million heartbeats I could pick 
out if I concentrated. My Blood Archon senses went into overdrive.

Too much life. Too much death. Too much everything pressed into one 
impossible space.

"Welcome home, Sir." Raechelle's hand found mine.

Home. Everyone kept using that word.

Why did it feel true?
```

### What Changed:
- 156 words → 115 words (26% reduction)
- 12 adjectives → 4 adjectives (67% cut)
- "Impossible splendor" → specific sensory details
- Added smell (underutilized sense)
- Blood Archon ability shown (heartbeats)
- Mystery reinforced (Home question)
- Fragments create disorientation
- Visceral over poetic
