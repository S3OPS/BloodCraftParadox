## Universal Techniques (Apply Everywhere)

### 1. The Dialogue Formula

**BAD Dialogue Indicators**:
- Starts with "Well, you see..."
- Contains "As you know..."
- Explains instead of reveals
- Uses complete, formal sentences
- Averages >20 words

**GOOD Dialogue Formula**:
```
Character A: [Short statement. Max 15 words.]
Character B: [Reaction. Fragment okay.]
Character A: [Follow-up or action.]
```

**Example Transformation**:
```
Before (54 words):
"I understand that you might be feeling overwhelmed by all of this information, 
but it's important that you comprehend the significance of your heritage and 
the responsibilities that come with it, as they will shape your future in ways 
you cannot yet imagine."

After (12 words):
"I know it's overwhelming. But you need to understand your heritage."

Even Better (8 words):
"Overwhelming, I know. But you need to understand."
```

### 2. The "Felt" Search & Destroy

**Process**:
1. Find every instance of "felt" + emotion word
2. Ask: "How does this show in the body?"
3. Replace with physical manifestation

**Examples**:
- "felt afraid" → "My hands wouldn't stop shaking"
- "felt angry" → "Heat flooded my face. My jaw clenched."
- "felt attracted" → "My mouth went dry. Couldn't look away."
- "felt sad" → "My throat closed up. Eyes burned."
- "felt excited" → "My pulse kicked up. Couldn't stand still."

### 3. The Adjective Audit

**Rule**: Keep only adjectives that add NEW information

**Test**: Remove the adjective. Does meaning change?
- "The old house" → Keep (age matters)
- "The ancient, crumbling, decrepit house" → Cut to "The crumbling house"
- "Her beautiful, flowing, lustrous hair" → Cut to "Her hair"

**Target**: 30-50% reduction in adjective count

### 4. The Purple Prose Blacklist

**Never Use These Words** (Find & Replace with specific details):
- Cascading, flowing, billowing → Describe actual movement
- Breathtaking, stunning, magnificent → Show reaction instead
- Pulsating, vibrating, throbbing → Unless literally doing so
- Tapestry, symphony, kaleidoscope → No metaphor stacking
- Ethereal, otherworldly, mystical → Show what's strange/different

**Replace With**:
- Specific measurements ("six feet tall" not "towering")
- Concrete textures ("rough bark" not "ancient wood")
- Named colors ("rust red" not "crimson hue")
- Actual sensations ("burned" not "warm tingle")

### 5. The Action Scene Speed-Up

**Current Formula** (Predicted):
- Long sentences describing each movement
- Multiple adjectives per action
- Character thoughts during combat
- Explained strategy

**Required Formula**:
```
He swung. I ducked. 

Fist connected. My ribs. Crack.

Pain. White-hot. Couldn't breathe.

Move. Had to move.
```

**Rules**:
- Sentence fragments during fast action
- <5 words per sentence during peak violence
- No adjectives unless critical ("broken" knife, "loaded" gun)
- No thought processes—only instinct/reaction

```
Riven finds evidence of Kaleth's identity
Information presented normally, calmly
He doesn't believe it yet
```

```
Without warning, a memory hits
NOT a flashback—a POSSESSION
```

### Act 3: Denial & Evidence (Mounting Dread)
```
Riven insists he's not Kaleth
But his actions betray him:
  - Speaking languages he doesn't know
  - Recognizing places he's never been  
  - Reacting to people with Kaleth's emotions
```

### Act 4: The Break (Psychological Horror Peak)
```
Full memory integration attempt
Riven's consciousness fragmenting
Voice shifts mid-sentence
Can't tell his memories from Kaleth's
```

```
Riven accepts he IS Kaleth reborn
But chooses to be Riven anyway
The memories stay (can't erase them)
The power stays (Kaleth's abilities)
But Riven's morality leads
Changed, not destroyed
```

```
As the sun began to rise over the horizon, painting the sky in hues of pink
```

**AFTER** (Stephen King style):
```
Dawn. Red sky through the trees. Raechelle waiting in the clearing.

"We start simple," she said. "Feel your own blood. The pulse in your wrist."

I focused. Felt nothing but my heartbeat.

"You're thinking too hard. Blood knows blood. Stop trying. Just listen."

I closed my eyes. Tuned out the birds, the breeze, my own thoughts.

There. A rhythm. Not my heartbeat—deeper. The flow itself. Every vein, every 
capillary, a river system mapped in sensation.

"Good." Her voice seemed far away. "Now push."

I pushed. Something in my chest lurched. The blood in my arm moved wrong—
upstream against the current. Pain shot through my bicep.

"Fuck!" I stumbled. The connection snapped.

"Again," Raechelle said. "Pain means you're learning."
```

**Analysis of Changes**:
- Dialogue: 40 words → 10 words average
- Exposition: Cut entirely
- Action: Immediate, physical
- Failure shown: Makes success earned
- Voice: Maintained Riven's modern tone
