## QUALITY CHECK AFTER EACH CHANGE

Ask:
1. Is this showing or telling?
2. Could I cut an adjective?
3. Is the dialogue natural?
4. Would a film director know what to shoot?
5. Am I explaining or experiencing?

If any answer is wrong, revise again.
