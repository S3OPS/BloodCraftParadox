## Stephen King Principles Applied

### 1. Show, Don't Tell
**Not**: "I felt terrified"  
**But**: "My hands wouldn't stop shaking"

### 2. Terse Dialogue
**Not**: 40-word formal speeches  
**But**: 10-15 word natural exchanges with fragments

### 3. Cut Purple Prose
**Not**: "Her hair cascaded like a waterfall of midnight silk"  
**But**: "Black hair. Loose. Falling past her shoulders."

### 4. Immediate Action
**Not**: Long buildup before revelations  
**But**: Bombshells delivered fast and hard

### 5. Visceral Sensory Details
**Not**: "It was unpleasant"  
**But**: "The scent hit me first. Copper and something older."

### 6. Natural Voice
**Not**: Literary perfection  
**But**: How real people (especially exhausted, traumatized people) actually talk

### 7. Ground Supernatural in Physical
**Not**: Abstract magic descriptions  
**But**: "My veins burned. The pressure built behind my eyes."
