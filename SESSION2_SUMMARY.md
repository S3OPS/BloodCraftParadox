## Stephen King Principles Applied

### 1. Show Physical Grief
**Instead of**: "I felt sad about my parents"
**We wrote**: 
- "The closet smelled like Mom"
- "My eyes burned"
- "Dad's coffee mug still on the counter from this morning"

### 2. Immediate Revelations
**Instead of**: Delaying journal discovery with exposition
**We wrote**: Direct path to journal, immediate opening, bombshell in first paragraph

### 3. Natural Exhausted Dialogue
**Instead of**: Complete sentences explaining magic theory
**We wrote**: 
- Fragments: "Can't. Every time I close my eyes, I see—"
- Interruptions: "I know."
- Commands: "Inside. Now."

### 4. Sensory Grounding
**Added**:
- Heightened senses from awakening
- Specific scents (jasmine, pine sap, lavender)
- Physical sensations (warm leather, cool gravel)
- Sound details (gravel crunch, keyboard clicks)

### 5. Cut Exposition Dumps
**Removed**:
- 40-acre property description
- House construction history  
- Detailed magical theory speeches
- Formal relationship explanations

**Replaced with**: Character-filtered observations, emotional beats, natural dialogue

- **Found and replaced**: 15+ instances in improved sections
- **Method**: Replace with physical manifestations
- **Example**: "I felt exhausted" → "The exhaustion hit me like a truck"

### Purple Prose Removal
**Eliminated terms**:
- "cascading," "tapestry," "vibrant," "breathtaking"
- "enchanting," "radiant," "magnificent," "ethereal"
- "pulsating," "shimmering," "glowing" (except where literally glowing)

## What Makes This "Stephen King Style"

### Horror Through Specificity
King doesn't write "it was scary." He writes exact details that create dread:
- "Three spikes. One through chest, abdomen, thigh."
- "I could hear insects in the trees, their legs scraping bark."
- "The closet smelled like Mom."

### Grounding Supernatural in Mundane
Magic is real, but:
- It happens next to Dad's coffee mug
- In a closet that smells like lavender
- While exhausted and wanting to sleep

### Characters Sound Like Real People
Not formal speeches, but:
- Exhausted fragments
- Self-deprecating humor ("Eloquent. Really selling the whole 'dominant' thing.")
- Natural interruptions and trailing off

### Grief Through Physical World
- Empty driver's seat
- Lights still on in the house
- Mug from this morning
- Smell of Mom's perfume
